# P5.1 — Messaging specification

Status: **written 2026-08-23; awaiting Adam's approval and citation check**
(PLAN §3 Q6, §11). Every rule below names the SAFEGUARDING.md invariant it
implements, and every invariant's citation row (SAFEGUARDING §2.3, C1–C9) is
Adam's to verify against the currently published FA Safeguarding Children
Policy and Cheshire FA guidance. Per SAFEGUARDING §2.3 the *mechanism* ships;
*sign-off* waits for the citations. Built as P5.2–P5.6 on that basis, with the
open items listed in §9.

## 1. Conversation types

| Type | Who creates | Participants | Minors may reply? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `dm` | any member with an account | exactly two genuine participants | yes | SG-1 applies in full; SG-1.4 (own guardian) and SG-1.9 (supervision-exempt) are the only adult↔minor 1:1 cases |
| `group` | any member | two or more, invited | yes | SG-1 applies after every join/leave; a group that shrinks to adult+minor is refused (SG-1.1) |
| `team` | system (P5.3) | live team members + guardians of minor players, synced from `team_memberships` and `guardianships` | yes | compliant by construction: every minor's guardians are in the room |
| `announcement` | team staff / club_admin | recipients cannot post; only the creator and staff can | **no** | excluded from SG-1 explicitly (SG-1.6) |

## 2. Participants

- `conversation_participants(conversation_id, person_id, joined_at, left_at,
  last_read_message_id, basis)` — `basis` ∈ `member`, `guardian`, `staff`,
  `creator`, `oversight`. `oversight` rows are never created: reads by the lead
  go through the SG-9 accessors and leave no participant row (SG-1.5a). A lead
  or admin who is a genuine participant has an ordinary basis and counts (SG-1.5b).
- Leaving is `left_at`; history is kept (SG-2, SG-8). Re-joining is a new row.
- Who may add a participant: the creator or any staff/admin for `group`;
  nobody by hand for `team` (P5.3 owns it); `announcement` recipients are set
  by the creator.
- Who may remove: the participant themself (subject to SG-1.1), the creator,
  staff, admin.

## 3. The SG-1 rule, as enforced

One STABLE function, `conversation_is_compliant(conversation_id)`, evaluates
SG-1's precise form over the active participant set, and is called by triggers
on `conversation_participants` (INSERT; UPDATE of `left_at`), `messages`
(INSERT — SG-1.7), `people` (UPDATE of `dob` — SG-1.2, carried by the single
`people_dob_guard()`), `guardianships` (DELETE / retarget / `ended_at` — SG-1.8,
completing P1.3's scaffold), `guardian_consents` (UPDATE of `revoked_at` —
SG-1.9) and `site_settings` (raising `safeguarding.unsupervised_messaging_min_age`
— SG-1.9), and by the nightly checker. Each refusal names the conversations.

Decisions already recorded that this spec inherits: D2 default (one adult +
two minors is permitted at DB level; P5.3 auto-includes guardians so it is
reachable only by guardians leaving, which SG-1.1 blocks); SG-1.9 as Adam
decided on 2026-08-22 (14+, with `unsupervised_messaging` consent, flagged
`supervised_by_lead`).

## 4. Messages

- `messages(conversation_id, sender_person_id, body, reply_to_id, created_at,
  deleted_at, deleted_by, redacted_at, redaction_reason)`; attachments are
  Supabase Storage objects under a private `attachments` bucket referenced by
  `message_attachments(message_id, storage_path, content_type, byte_size)`,
  reached only by signed URLs with the same ≤15-minute TTL as media (SG-5/Q4).
- Soft delete only (SG-2): `deny_hard_delete()` + `deny_truncate()` on
  `messages`, `conversation_participants`, `message_attachments`, with
  DELETE/TRUNCATE revoked from all three API roles. A sender may soft-delete
  their own message; staff/admin may soft-delete any in conversations they are
  in; the lead may *redact* (SG-8) any message in a conversation involving a
  minor, through an audited accessor.
- Editing is not supported (an edit is a new message; the old one may be
  soft-deleted). Simpler, and the audit trail is honest.
- `announcement` conversations refuse posts from non-staff participants.

## 5. Visibility and oversight (SG-9)

- Participant-scoped policies only. No "admin reads all" policy exists.
- `read_conversation_as_lead(conversation_id, reason)` and
  `export_conversation_as_lead(conversation_id, reason)`: `safeguarding_lead`
  or `club_admin`; mandatory non-blank reason; only conversations that have
  ever had a minor participant; audit row (`messaging.conversation.admin_read`
  / `.export`, `{reason, message_count, includes_minor}`) written before
  returning, even when empty; never a participant row.
- `supervised_by_lead` is set by the admitting trigger and cannot be cleared
  while a minor participant is active. Every participant of such a
  conversation sees a persistent banner (P5.4 web, P6.3 mobile — acceptance
  criteria, not DB invariants). The consent screen's notice version is
  recorded on the consent row (P1.7 `notice_version`).

## 6. Realtime, read receipts, typing, push

- Web/mobile subscribe to `messages` by conversation through Supabase
  Realtime (RLS-filtered). Read receipts are `last_read_message_id` on the
  participant row (self-updatable). Typing is a Realtime broadcast channel,
  never stored.
- Push fan-out (P5.5): a DB webhook on `messages` INSERT → Edge Function →
  `enqueue_message(channel = 'push', category = 'transactional')` per offline
  participant (P4.4 preferences: push may be turned off per person; muted
  conversations — `conversation_participants.muted_until` — are skipped).
  Push payloads carry the conversation id and sender name, **never the body**
  for conversations with a minor (a lock-screen is not a participant).

## 7. Retention (SG-8, D7)

Periods are D7's placeholders: message bodies in ordinary conversations
redacted after 24 months; never while an attached concern is open or a
`legal_hold` is set on the conversation, a participant, or a message. The
retention job (P5.6) ships **disabled, dry-run**, logging what it would
redact, until Adam signs D7 off. `audit_log` is never redacted.

## 8. Moderation and export (P5.6)

- Report a message: any participant → `report_message(message_id, reason)`
  creates a `safeguarding_concerns` row via `report_concern()` (SG-3) with the
  message id in the narrative's structured prefix, so the lead works the case
  in one place.
- Export: `export_conversation_as_lead()` returns the full history including
  soft-deleted and redacted rows (with redaction markers), attachments as
  signed URLs, audit-logged.
- Close/archive: `conversations.closed_at`; load-bearing for SG-1.8 and SG-1.9
  (a closed conversation no longer needs its guardian or consent).
