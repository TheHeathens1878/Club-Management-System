## Schema

| Object | Notes |
|---|---|
| `team_fulltime_links` | One row per team (PK `team_id`): `source_url` (must be `fulltime.thefa.com`), `league_id`, `ft_season_id`, `division_id`, `fixture_group_key`, optional `ft_team_id`/`ft_team_name`, `enabled`, last import outcome (`ok`/`error`/`challenge`, count, error). **Re-linking is an UPDATE of this row; fixtures are keyed by `(team_id, external_ref)` and are never touched**, so a new season or league change orphans nothing (tested). RLS: `club_admin` only; the importer uses `service_role`. |
| `fixtures` | team × season; `opponent` text (not a FK — opponents are not people or teams the club manages); `is_home`; `competition`; `kickoff_at timestamptz`; `status` (scheduled/postponed/cancelled/played/abandoned); `source` (fulltime/manual); `external_ref` (Full-Time's `displayFixture.html?id=` — required for fulltime rows, immutable once imported, unique per team); `venue_resource_id` → a **pitch** in `resources` (guard refuses a function room); `venue_text`; paired scores; `imported_at`/`last_seen_at`. |
| `availability` | (fixture, person) → available/unavailable/maybe + note; guard requires a live membership on the fixture's team for its season; `set_by` stamped. |
| `selections` | (fixture, person) starter/substitute + shirt number; guard requires a live **player** membership (a coach cannot be selected; selection is not membership and carries no SG-6 weight). |
| helpers | `fixture_team_id()`, `is_team_member()`, `is_team_guardian()` (active guardian of a minor on the team), `can_act_for(person)` (self, or active guardian of a minor). |

## RLS

- `fixtures`: any authenticated person reads (a fixture list is club-public);
  `club_admin` and the team's staff (`is_team_staff`, P2.1) insert/update;
  `club_admin` deletes.
- `availability`: read by self/guardian, team staff, admins; written by
  self/guardian (`can_act_for`), team staff, `club_admin`.
- `selections`: read by live team members, guardians of minors on the team,
  staff, admins; written by team staff and `club_admin`.
- `team_fulltime_links`: `club_admin` only. No anon anywhere.

## The Full-Time integration (PLAN §3 Q2)

Verified against live pages on 2026-08-23: identifiers are `league`,
`selectedSeason`, `selectedDivision`, `selectedCompetition`,
`selectedFixtureGroupKey`, with `selectedTeam` on the fixtures form and
`displayTeam.html?id=` / `displayFixture.html?id=` for teams and fixtures.
**Cloudflare serves a "Just a moment…" challenge after a few requests** and a
403 to non-browser fetchers, so the parser classifies responses
(`ok`/`challenge`/`not_found`/`error`), the importer backs off and records
`last_import_status = 'challenge'`, and the admin screen says so plainly. The
manual paste/CSV fallback is therefore not optional (P2.4).
