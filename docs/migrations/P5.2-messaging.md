## SG-1, one function, six trigger sites

`conversation_is_compliant(conversation_id, …hypothetical params)` evaluates
SG-1's precise form. Called from: participants INSERT / UPDATE OF left_at (an
AFTER constraint trigger, per row, which also **sets `supervised_by_lead`**
when the exemption is the only thing making the set compliant — SG-1.9);
messages INSERT (SG-1.7); the single `people_dob_guard()` (SG-1.2);
`guardianships_conversation_guard()` — P1.3's scaffold body replaced (SG-1.8:
delete / retarget / **end** → reject unless every affected conversation is
closed); `guardian_consents` revocation and a raised
`unsupervised_messaging_min_age` (SG-1.9, reject-unless-closed); and
`sg1_nightly_check()`. Hypotheticals are evaluated honestly (the pair to ignore,
the consent to treat as revoked, the new age, the new dob).

Two NULL-handling defects were found by the tests and fixed before rehearsal:
an `= NULL` inside a `not (...)` silently dropped the guardian match. Worth
remembering for every future guard.

## SG-9

`read_conversation_as_lead` / `export_conversation_as_lead` (+
`redact_message_as_lead`): lead or club_admin, mandatory reason, only
conversations that have ever had a minor, audit before returning (even when
empty), never a participant row. No admin read policy exists; the test proves
the lead reads zero rows directly.

## SG-2

Soft delete only on messages / participants / attachments; DELETE/TRUNCATE
revoked from all three API roles; body/sender immutable; deletion cannot be
undone; redaction replaces the body.

## P5.3 team rooms

`team` + `announcement` conversation per team per season, created on demand;
membership synced from `team_memberships` (staff/member basis) and, for
minors, every active guardian (basis guardian) — **guardians are inserted
first** because the SG-1 check runs per row. Leaving marks `left_at`
(history kept); guardians leave when no live minor child of theirs remains;
new/ended guardianships sync too. Backfilled for live memberships.

## P5.6 tooling

`report_message(message, reason)` → a `safeguarding_concerns` row via SG-3's
`report_concern()` with a structured `[message:… conversation:…]` prefix;
retention: `retention.messages_months` (24) and `retention.enabled`
(**false** — D7), `message_retention_candidates()`, `retention_run(dry_run)`
which is forced to dry-run while disabled, skips legal holds and open
concerns, redacts-not-deletes, never touches `audit_log`. Export completeness
(incl. soft-deleted) is tested.
